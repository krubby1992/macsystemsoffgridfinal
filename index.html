Mac Systems provides installation, repair, troubleshooting and maintenance services for off-grid homes, cabins, and remote properties.

Based on Lasqueti Island, Mac Systems serves property owners throughout the Gulf Islands with practical, dependable solutions built for real-world off-grid living.

With training in electromechanics, solar energy systems, and technical systems integration—including studies through Rosemount Technology Centre, Camosun College, and NABCEP Associate training—the focus is on reliable work that keeps essential systems running smoothly.

From power generation and water systems to automation, monitoring, security, heating, and general property infrastructure, Mac Systems helps remote properties operate safely, efficiently, and independently.
